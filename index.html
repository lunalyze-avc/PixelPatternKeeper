How it works & shortcuts
  • Rows are counted from the starting corner (bottom-left or bottom-right, set in Pattern settings). In C2C, row 1 is that single corner square; in straight rows it's the bottom line.
  • The strip shows the current row laid flat; numbers are the order you work them. Tap a square to toggle it.
  • “Start/End: increase or decrease” tells you whether that end of the row grows or shrinks compared with the last row.
  • N / Enter stitch done · Backspace undo stitch · [ ] previous/next row
  • P hide/show these panels · R turn diagonal · F fit · +/− zoom · hold Space to drag
  • To remove a single ✓, use Unmark, tap the square in the row strip, or tap it again with Mark done.
  • To add or remove rows and columns, open Pattern settings: add or remove them at the top, right, bottom or left, or use “Add or delete one in the middle…” and tap a square.
  • The color box next to Paint/Fill/Pick is a color picker: choose any color to paint with (new colors are added to your list). ▾ picks one of the pattern's colors.
  • H move · M mark · U unmark · B paint · G fill · I pick · Ctrl+Z undo
